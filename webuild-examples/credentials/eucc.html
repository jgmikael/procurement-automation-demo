🔗 EBWV Vocabulary Mappings

All attributes use European Business Wallet Vocabulary (EBWV) v0.1 as defined at https://w3id.org/ebwv/

ebwv:legalName → Legal person name
ebwv:legalIdentifier → EUID structure
ebwv:legalForm → Legal form type
ebwv:registeredAddress → Official address
ebwv:dateOfRegistration → Registration date
ebwv:legalStatus → Company status
ebwv:activity → NACE classification
ebwv:legalRepresentative → Authorized representatives
ebwv:subscribedCapital → Share capital
ebwv:contactPoint → Digital contact information
