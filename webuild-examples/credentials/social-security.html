⚠️ Qualification Note
Important Limitations
The attestation confirms the compliance status recorded by URSSAF for the covered obligations at the valid-as-of date (2026-09-01). It does not replace any separate attestation required for:
  • Obligations outside the stated scope (e.g., specific industry contributions)
  • Obligations outside URSSAF jurisdiction (e.g., other French regions)
  • Future periods after the assessment date
  • International operations or subsidiaries
This attestation is valid for administrative and procurement purposes requiring proof of social security compliance in France.

⚠️ Verification Requirements
Relying parties MUST:
  • Verify attestation signature using URSSAF trust anchor
  • Check attestation expiration date (typical validity: 6 months)
  • Verify valid-as-of date is recent enough for use case
  • Confirm compliance status is "upToDate" or acceptable alternative
  • Request and verify holder's PID to confirm employer identity
