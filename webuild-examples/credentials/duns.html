🔢 What is a DUNS Number?
The Data Universal Numbering System (DUNS) is a proprietary 9-digit identifier issued by Dun & Bradstreet to uniquely identify business entities worldwide.

Purpose:
  • Unique identification of legal entities, sites, and locations
  • Enable standardized business verification across industries
  • Support credit assessment and risk management
  • Required for supplier qualification in many industries
  • Facilitate KYS (Know Your Supplier) and KYC processes

Coverage: Over 400 million business entities globally
📋 DUNS Attestation Use Cases
This DUNS attestation enables:
  • Supplier Onboarding: Verify supplier identity and access D&B database
  • Credit Assessment: Link to Dun & Bradstreet credit reports and ratings
  • Government Contracts: Required for US federal government contracting
  • Industry Compliance: Many sectors require DUNS for business relationships
  • Supply Chain Mapping: Track suppliers across global supply chains
  • Risk Management: Access financial health and business viability data
  • KYS/KYC Processes: Streamlined due diligence verification
🔍 DUNS vs Other Identifiers
How DUNS compares to other business identifiers:
  • DUNS: Proprietary (Dun & Bradstreet), global coverage, credit data integration
  • LEI: ISO standard, focused on financial markets, regulatory requirement
  • EUID: EU-specific, based on national business registers
  • VAT ID: Tax-focused, EU cross-border transactions
  • GLN: GS1 standard, supply chain and logistics focus

DUNS Advantage: Access to comprehensive D&B database with financial ratings, corporate family trees, and risk indicators.
📊 Three Types of DUNS
Dun & Bradstreet issues DUNS numbers for three entity types:
  • Legal Entities: The primary legal organization (this example)
  • Sites: Physical operational locations of the legal entity
  • Locations: Additional addresses or facilities

This attestation represents a Legal Entity DUNS - the top-level organization identifier.

🔒 DUNS as Complementary Identifier
The DUNS Legal Entity Attestation complements the EUCC by providing the DUNS number for a legal entity. With this number, an EBW owner can:
  • Access the Dun & Bradstreet database for detailed company information
  • Request non-core identity attributes (financial ratings, corporate structure)
  • Obtain credit reports and business risk assessments
  • Verify corporate family trees and ownership structures

This attestation can be self-issued by the legal entity or issued by an authorized competent body based on official D&B registry data.

📊 Data Model
The DUNS attestation is structured as follows:

├─ duns_number (tstr) - 9-digit unique identifier
├─ legal_entity (Object)
│   ├─ legal_name (tstr) - Registered legal name
│   ├─ legal_form (tstr) - Legal form (GmbH, AG, Ltd, etc.)
│   └─ address (Address) - Registered address
│       ├─ street (tstr)
│       ├─ nr (tstr)
│       ├─ postal_code (tstr)
│       ├─ city (tstr)
│       └─ country (tstr) - ISO 3166-1 alpha-2
🔒 Selective Disclosure Attributes
Mandatory attributes (NOT selectively disclosable):
  • duns_number - 9-digit DUNS identifier
  • legal_entity.legal_name - Registered legal name
  • legal_entity.legal_form - Legal form type
  • legal_entity.address - Registered address object

Optional address sub-fields (selectively disclosable):
  • All address components (street, nr, postal_code, city, country)
