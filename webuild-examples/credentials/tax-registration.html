📜 What is a Tax Registration Certificate?
A Tax Registration Certificate is an official document issued by a national tax authority that verifies a business entity's registration for tax purposes.

Purpose:
  • Confirms the entity is registered with the tax authority
  • Provides the unique tax registration number
  • Enables tax reporting and compliance verification
  • Required for business operations and contracts
  • Complements VAT registration for comprehensive tax status

Difference from VAT ID: Tax Registration covers broader corporate tax obligations, while VAT ID specifically addresses value-added tax for cross-border transactions.
📋 Tax Registration Attestation Use Cases
This Tax Registration Certificate attestation enables:
  • Business Verification: Confirm tax compliance for contracts and partnerships
  • Public Procurement: Verify tax registration status for tender eligibility
  • KYB (Know Your Business): Due diligence for financial institutions
  • Supplier Onboarding: Validate tax standing for supply chain integration
  • Cross-Border Trade: Demonstrate tax compliance in international transactions
  • Regulatory Reporting: Provide verified tax data to authorities
  • Exclusion Grounds: Prove no grounds for exclusion due to unpaid taxes
🔍 Tax Registration vs VAT ID
Tax Registration Certificate:
  • Broader corporate tax obligations
  • Corporate income tax, trade tax, solidarity surcharge
  • National tax authority registration
  • Required for all business entities

VAT ID:
  • Specific to value-added tax
  • EU cross-border transactions focus
  • Separate registration and number
  • Optional depending on turnover/activities

Both are needed for comprehensive tax compliance verification in public procurement and business relationships.
🇩🇪 German Tax Registration System
Steuernummer (Tax Number):
  • Issued by local tax office (Finanzamt)
  • Format: XX/XXX/XXXXX (varies by federal state)
  • Used for corporate tax, trade tax, and payroll tax
  • Required for all business operations in Germany

Key German Business Taxes:
  • Körperschaftsteuer: Corporate income tax (15%)
  • Gewerbesteuer: Trade tax (varies by municipality)
  • Solidaritätszuschlag: Solidarity surcharge (5.5% of corporate tax)

🌍 National Tax Systems
Member State Tax Registration Variations:

Each EU Member State has its own tax registration system:
  • Germany: Steuernummer (local) + Steuer-ID (federal)
  • France: SIREN (company) + SIRET (establishment)
  • Netherlands: RSIN (legal entities)
  • Spain: NIF (Número de Identificación Fiscal)
  • Italy: Codice Fiscale

The attestation structure accommodates these variations through the taxNumber field and taxJurisdiction country code.
