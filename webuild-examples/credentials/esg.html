🔗 EBWV Vocabulary Mappings

ESG Certificate uses EBWV for legal entity identification and extends it with certification-specific attributes.

Legal Entity (EBWV-based):
ebwv:legalName → Legal person name
ebwv:legalForm → Legal form type
ebwv:legalIdentifier → EUID, LEI, etc.
ebwv:registeredAddress → Primary address

ESG Extensions:
esg:certificateType → ISO standard
esg:certificateVersion → Standard version
esg:registrationNumber → Unique cert ID
esg:scope → Certified sites and activities
esg:evidence → Original certificate

📚 ISO 14001:2015 Environmental Management

ISO 14001:2015 specifies requirements for an environmental management system that an organization can use to enhance its environmental performance.

Key Requirements:

  • Environmental policy and objectives
  • Identification of environmental aspects and impacts
  • Legal and other requirements compliance
  • Operational planning and controls
  • Emergency preparedness and response
  • Monitoring, measurement, analysis, and evaluation
  • Management review and continual improvement

🏗️ Procurement Use Cases

Green Public Procurement:

  • Eligibility Criteria: Require ISO 14001 for environmental-sensitive tenders
  • Award Criteria: Additional points for certified environmental management
  • Site Verification: Check if specific facility is within certified scope
  • Supply Chain: Verify environmental practices of subcontractors
  • Automated Checks: Machine-readable certificate data enables instant verification

Example Procurement Rule:

IF supplier.hasCertificate("ISO14001") AND
   supplier.certificate.validUntil > tender.deadline AND
   supplier.certificate.scope.covers(tender.deliverySite)
THEN supplier.eligible = true
