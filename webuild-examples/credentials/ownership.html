🧬 BODS + EBWV Semantic Foundation

The Ownership Attestation leverages the Beneficial Ownership Data Standard (BODS) v0.4 vocabulary and terminology, aligned with AMLR 2024/1624 requirements.

📚 Key Standards

  • BODS v0.4: Beneficial Ownership Data Standard - structure and taxonomy for ownership disclosure
  • AMLR 2024/1624: EU Anti-Money Laundering Regulation - legal requirements for beneficial ownership transparency
  • EBWV v0.1: European Business Wallet Vocabulary - semantic properties for business attributes
  • ISO 17442: Legal Entity Identifier (LEI) standard
  • ISO 3166-1: Country codes (alpha-2)
  • ISO 8601: Date format (YYYY-MM-DD)

🎯 Selective Disclosure

Each owner entry is individually selectively disclosable in SD-JWT format:

owner[0] (Person) owner[1] (Legal Entity) owner[2] (Legal Arrangement)

🔒 Privacy-Preserving Presentation

The holder can selectively disclose owner entries based on Relying Party requirements:

  • Scenario 1 (AML/KYC): Disclose only Person owners (≥25% threshold) → verify beneficial ownership
  • Scenario 2 (Due Diligence): Disclose all owners → comprehensive ownership transparency
  • Scenario 3 (Conflict of Interest): Disclose specific entity owners → check for related parties
  • Scenario 4 (Audit Trail): Disclose ownership + evidence → verify source documentation
