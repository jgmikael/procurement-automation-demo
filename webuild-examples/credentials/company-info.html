🎯 Selective Disclosure

The following claims are individually selectively disclosable in SD-JWT format:

number_of_employees trade_alias[] previous_legal_name[] financial_statements financial_statements.facts[]

🔒 Privacy-Preserving Presentation

The holder can selectively disclose only the attributes requested by the Relying Party:

  • Scenario 1 (Supplier Onboarding): Disclose employee count + EUID → verify company size category
  • Scenario 2 (Credit Assessment): Disclose financial facts (Revenue, Equity) → assess creditworthiness
  • Scenario 3 (Due Diligence): Disclose all attributes → comprehensive KYC/KYS verification
  • Scenario 4 (Simple Verification): Disclose only legal entity info → verify company identity
