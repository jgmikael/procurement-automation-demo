Key Difference from NCBV Analysis:

EBWV focuses on legal representatives (statutory roles registered in business registers).
For broader mandate/authorization concepts, EBWV v0.1 does not yet include explicit "Mandate" or "PowerOfAttorney" classes.

The EBWV Semantic Analysis project explored these concepts comparatively, but EBWV v0.1 currently implements only LegalRepresentative.
