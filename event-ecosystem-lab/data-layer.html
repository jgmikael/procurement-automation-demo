All issuers, companies, identifiers and statements are invented. No proofs or status services are supplied, so these are unsigned examples, not cryptographically verifiable credentials.

The construction sequence

From source meaning to portable evidence.

EBWV supplies business concepts, ePO supplies procurement concepts, and a small UBL 2.4 document vocabulary reuses selected terms from Finland’s busdoc Turtle export. SHACL specifies each subject’s required data.

01 / SHARED TERMS

EBWV + ePO

The eligibility examples use ebwv:Company, ebwv:legalName and ebwv:legalIdentifier for the operator, and link their procedure scope to epo:Lot. ePO’s exclusion grounds and selection criteria guide the profile; the extension terms are local to this demo.

02 / TRADE VOCABULARY

UBL 2.4 + busdoc → OWL

Eight UBL document roots link to selected busdoc component classes and properties. The ninth case, a procurement award decision, uses ePO and sits outside the Trade OWL. The Turtle export reflects UBL 2.2 components; this limited profile checks selected UBL 2.4 counterparts and does not assert OWL equivalence to XML.

03 / CONSTRAINTS

SHACL → VC

Each fixture has a subject with a matching sh:targetClass shape, required paths, types and selected value sets. The W3C VC envelope adds issuer, subject and validity fields. Signature, trust and legal assessment are separate checks.

Sources and design limits
What each layer actually says.
Eligibility conditions
Commission proposal COM(2026) 590, Articles 25–29 and 133: exclusion grounds, selection criteria, eligibility evidence and proposed electronic eligibility service. The particular attestations and statuses shown here are illustrative profiles; the proposal has not enacted these VC schemas.
Proposal ↗
Business and procurement semantics
EBWV v0.1 (Webuild draft) and the EU Publications Office ePO repository. The demo extension uses its own resolvable namespace for terms missing from these vocabularies. rdfs:seeAlso indicates conceptual relevance, not identity.
EBWV ↗ · ePO ↗
EU Company Certificate
The registration example follows the WE BUILD EUCC rulebook’s limited liability company variant, including mandatory company data and representation rules. It uses EBWV terms and a small local extension for the explicit joint-signature group and required count. The rulebook is a consortium draft, and this fixture is unsigned.
WE BUILD EUCC rulebook ↗
Commercial syntax
OASIS UBL 2.4 document roots: Catalogue, Quotation, OrderResponse, Order, Invoice, DespatchAdvice, ReceiptAdvice and Waybill. Selected components reuse the busdoc Turtle export from Finland’s Interoperability Platform. The award decision uses ePO; the seller’s OrderResponse answers the subsequent order. Peppol implementation rules and UBL XML conformance are outside these SHACL checks.
busdoc ↗ · UBL 2.4 ↗
Credential format
W3C VC Data Model 2.0. All sample issuers use fictional URNs and the credentials omit a proof. Subject validation by SHACL does not establish authenticity or legal eligibility. Actual deployment needs controlled issuers, proof suites, credential status, code lists, XML mappings and procedure-specific rules.
W3C VC 2.0 ↗
