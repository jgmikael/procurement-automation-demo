The construction sequence

From source meaning to portable evidence.

EBWV supplies business concepts, ePO supplies procurement concepts, and a deliberately small demo vocabulary describes trade messages drawn from Peppol and UBL. SHACL specifies each subject’s required data.

01 / SHARED TERMS

EBWV + ePO

The eligibility examples use ebwv:Company, ebwv:legalName and ebwv:legalIdentifier for the operator, and link their procedure scope to epo:Lot. ePO’s exclusion grounds and selection criteria guide the profile; the extension terms are local to this demo.

02 / TRADE VOCABULARY

Peppol + UBL → OWL

Nine document classes carry a small set of fields: document number, parties, amounts, items and references. They cite source syntaxes as provenance. They do not claim OWL equivalence to XML or replace Peppol conformance rules.

03 / CONSTRAINTS

SHACL → VC

Each fixture has a subject with a matching sh:targetClass shape, required paths, types and selected value sets. The W3C VC envelope adds issuer, subject and validity fields. Signature, trust and legal assessment are separate checks.

Sources and design limits
What each layer actually says.
Eligibility conditions
Commission proposal COM(2026) 590, Articles 25–29 and 133: exclusion grounds, selection criteria, eligibility evidence and proposed electronic eligibility service. The particular attestations and statuses shown here are illustrative profiles; the proposal has not enacted these VC schemas.
Proposal ↗
Business and procurement semantics
EBWV v0.1 (Webuild draft) and the EU Publications Office ePO repository. The demo extension uses its own resolvable namespace for terms missing from these vocabularies. rdfs:seeAlso indicates conceptual relevance, not identity.
EBWV ↗ · ePO ↗
Commercial syntax
Peppol BIS Catalogue, Ordering, Billing and Despatch Advice; OASIS UBL Quotation, ReceiptAdvice and Waybill. The quotation is distinct from an ePO tender. The buyer’s award decision accepts the tender; the seller’s order agreement accepts the subsequent order.
Peppol BIS ↗ · UBL 2.4 ↗
Credential format
W3C VC Data Model 2.0. All sample issuers use fictional URNs and the credentials omit a proof. Subject validation by SHACL does not establish authenticity or legal eligibility. Actual deployment needs controlled issuers, proof suites, credential status, code lists, XML mappings and procedure-specific rules.
W3C VC 2.0 ↗
Rebuild fixtures with python event-ecosystem-lab/build_data.py. The generated JSON, OWL and SHACL files are committed alongside the browser.
