    Evidence and design boundaries
    What is proposed, and what is added here
    Commission proposal
    COM(2026) 590 proposes a procurement interoperability network, digital business credential tools, an electronic eligibility service and semantic repository arrangements. Articles 28–29 and 128–133 inform this scenario. It is a proposal, not enacted law.
    Read the Commission proposal ↗
    Implementation extension
    EBWV-aligned evidence profiles, JSON-LD examples, rule APIs, AI agent orchestration and the wider lifecycle are design choices for this lab. They are not prescribed by the procurement proposal. The wallet regulation is also a proposal.
    Read the Business Wallet proposal ↗
    Dubai as a parallel direction
    Digital Dubai calls for integrated citizen and business services, connected and governed data, and agentic AI. This supports the architectural direction; it does not imply that Dubai has adopted EBWV, OWL or this procurement model.
